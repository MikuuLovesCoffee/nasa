EMBER ATLAS · TEAM AINEXA · XGBOOST, TRAINED OFFLINE

~45 s. The calendar tells you what's normal. The hard part is the year that breaks the pattern. So we trained a model, offline, on 25 years of unified fire history plus NASA POWER weather, forest loss, tree cover and vegetation greenness. We also found that wind matters together with dryness: in forests, a windy and dry month has 1.4 times the usual chance of big fire. It gives fire risk for this month and next for every one-degree cell, and explains why: for example, "hotter and drier than usual, and in years with weather like this, this place burned". On fires in places and months that usually don't burn, it's 73 percent more precise than "same as usual", tested on 2023 to 2025, years it never saw.
