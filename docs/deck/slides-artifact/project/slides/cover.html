~30 s. Hi, we're Team Ainexa from Uzbekistan. Every year, satellites spot millions of fires: wildfires, and farmers burning their fields, a lot of it right here in Central Asia. NASA has tracked these hotspots for 25 years. But ask the data a simple question, like "When does my area burn, and is this year unusual?", and it can't answer. We built Ember Atlas to answer exactly that.
