EMBER ATLAS · TEAM AINEXA

~50 s. So we taught MODIS to speak VIIRS. We took all 392 million detections from 2000 to 2025, kept vegetation fires, and counted them on a half-degree grid every month. Then, from the years both satellites flew together, we learned a VIIRS-to-MODIS ratio for every 5-degree region and every season. The key is honesty: we fitted only on 2012 to 2018, then predicted 2019 to 2025 and compared with real VIIRS. Monthly global error fell from 76 percent to 7.4. Per region, R-squared went from 0.50 to 0.81. One record, one scale, 25 years.
