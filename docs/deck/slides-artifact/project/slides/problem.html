~45 s. Here's why. The record comes from two instruments. MODIS, since 2000, with one-kilometre pixels. VIIRS, since 2012, with 375-metre pixels. VIIRS sees smaller fires, so it reports about four times more detections. Put the two records together as they are and you get this grey line: fire seems to explode in 2012. That never happened. The ruler changed. So nobody can honestly compare the 2005 season with 2024, and that comparison is exactly what early warning needs. The orange line is our fix.
