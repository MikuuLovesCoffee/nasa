~60 s. That gives us what the challenge asks for: a burning-activity calendar. Pick any country or province, or draw a box. Here's Uzbekistan: every row is a year, every column a month. You see the critical period at once: March to October holds 90 percent of the burning, so the advice for responders is "get ready by February". The outlined cells are unusual months, each compared with that month's own 25-year normal. July 2003 had three times the usual fire. We also test the trend: minus 1.8 percent a year, but not statistically significant, so we don't claim it. Every number downloads as CSV, and every view is a shareable link.
